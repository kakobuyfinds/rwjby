Check sizing, QC photos, and shipping

Match measurements to something you own

For clothing, measure a similar garment laid flat and compare chest width, length, and sleeves with the seller’s chart. For shoes, check whether the chart uses foot length or insole length before comparing.

Review photos of your exact variant

If your agent offers warehouse QC photos, check the size label, color, seams, and visible defects. Ask for a close-up or a tape-measure photo if the standard views leave a question. Check the agent’s return window before approving shipment.

Compare total cost before paying for shipping

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any agent or payment fees, and international freight. Check whether the quote includes applicable taxes and duties, and whether the carrier bills on actual or volumetric weight.

Example budget. A ¥105 item, ¥10 domestic delivery, ¥8 in fees, and ¥85 in estimated international freight come to ¥208 before any additional taxes or duties. The shipping figure is illustrative.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product listings come from?

The products and CNY price snapshots come from HipobuyFind. Each card links to its source listing. This page is an independent buying guide.

Can I order every listing through ACBuy?

Check the original marketplace link with ACBuy. Eligibility depends on the listing, available variants, and the shipping options for your destination.

Why is the checkout price different?

The seller may charge different prices for different variants or update a listing after the catalog snapshot. Exchange rates, fees, and shipping can also change the total.
